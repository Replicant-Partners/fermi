## What we agreed yesterday-into-today

Working forward to **Spec 25 — Forecast Relationship Groups**
(`docs/specs/25_FORECAST_RELATIONSHIP_GROUPS.md`).

### Decisions locked

1. **Path B**: forecast carries `relationship_groups: TEXT[]`. Groups
   declared as their own rows in `forecast_relationship_groups`.
   Membership is dynamic (queried via GIN-indexed array @> match).
2. **Three v1 kinds**: `mutex`, `at_most_n`, `implies`.
3. **Groups orthogonal to portfolios** — a forecast can be in both
   independently.
4. **Explicit console affordance** for declaring + managing groups
   (operator-facing UI, not just API/script).
5. **Operator-gate everywhere**: nothing mutates probabilities without
   an explicit click. Apply, Undo, Re-queue, Dismiss are all human-
   driven.
6. **Undo + Re-queue + portfolio-level rebalance view + per-forecast
   inherited-cascade bar** all required.

   API/script-only.

   required.

   else.

   apply/dismiss.

   WC, non-tournament forecasts. No domain-coupling.

   "now that we've seen two teams resolve, how does their exit cascade
   on probabilities — I want a general purpose way to do this."
8. **Mass-conserving redistribution**: stronger survivors absorb more
   of the eliminated mass. Ranking-preserving.
