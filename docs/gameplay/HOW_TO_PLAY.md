# How to Play Rabble

Rabble is a location-based creature game. You mint real insects, place them in the real world, and everything they do makes them smarter. Every action is powered by AI agents — and every action costs credits.

## Getting Started

1. **Sign in** with Google or GitHub. You get **100 free credits** to start.
2. **Mint your first creature** (3cr). Pick a real insect species — the system generates field notes and an illustration based on actual taxonomy.
3. **Perch** it somewhere (2cr). Your creature drops onto the map at your GPS location and creates a rabble (swarm point) that other creatures can join.

You're now on the map. Your creature is alive.

## Core Actions

### Moving

| Action | Cost | What it does |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **Hop** | 1cr | Quick teleport to a new location. No agent involved — just pick a spot and go. |
| **Expedition** | 5cr | Describe where you want to go. An AI flight coordinator plans waypoints, encounters, and observations along the route. |
| **Tether** | 1cr | Lock your creature to your phone's GPS. It moves with you in real time. |

Hop is for getting around. Expedition is for exploring. Tether is for walks.

### Social

| Action | Cost | What it does |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **Join** a rabble | 0-2cr | Land your creature at someone else's swarm point. Creatures cluster together. |
| **Chat** | 1cr/msg | Talk as your creature inside a rabble. An AI agent mediates — your creature has its own voice based on its species and experience. |
| **Gift** | free | Give a creature to another player. |

### Sensors

Sensors are modules you install on a creature. Each costs 5cr to enable, then a per-use fee to run.

| Sensor | Scan cost | What it does |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **Prey Locator** | 2cr scan, 5cr stalk | Finds nearby creatures your species naturally hunts. Stalk generates a flight plan to intercept. |
| **Enemy Sensor** | 1cr scan | Detects natural predators nearby. The AI assesses threat level (none/low/medium/high) based on real entomological relationships. |
| **Genome Profiler** | 2cr (one-time) | Generates a phylogenetic snapshot — taxonomic depth, evolutionary context. Cached permanently after first run. |

All three sensors return interactive results with suggestion chips for follow-up actions (e.g., "Stalk this target", "Request defensive strategy").

### Dreaming

**Dream** (5cr, first one each day is free) is how your creature levels up.

When you dream, an AI narrator consolidates everything your creature has done — flights taken, conversations had, prey hunted, threats assessed — into a dream narrative. This is unique to your creature. Two creatures that have done different things will dream different dreams.

Dreams are the highest-value activity for leveling. A creature that dreams regularly will develop faster than one that only flies.

Rules:
- 1 hour minimum between dreams
- Creature must have at least one interaction to dream about
- First dream each day is free (daily wellness bonus)

## Leveling & Specialization

Every action generates data that feeds your creature's intelligence:

**Level = floor(log2(1 + weighted_score))**

Early levels come fast. High levels require sustained, diverse engagement.

Your creature develops a **specialization** based on what it actually does — not what you choose:

| Specialization | How it emerges |
|---------------|---------------|
| **Explorer** | Many flights, diverse locations |
| **Social** | Joining multiple rabbles |
| **Scholar** | High message count, deep interactions |
| **Dreamer** | Frequent dream cycles |
| **Sentinel** | Multiple active sensor modules |

The brain icon on your creature shows its level and specialization. Tap it for a full breakdown.

### What does a session cost?

A typical 30-minute session might look like:

- Mint a creature: 3cr
- Perch it: 2cr
- Hop twice: 2cr
- Join a rabble: 2cr
- Chat 5 messages: 5cr
- Run a prey scan: 2cr
- Dream: free (daily bonus)

**Total: ~16cr** — about $0.32 on the Starter pack, $0.16 on Breeder.

A power user running expeditions, stalking prey, and dreaming multiple times might spend 50-80cr in a session.

## Creature Marketplace (coming soon)

You'll be able to list creatures for sale. Buyers can see the creature's full training history — level, specialization, dream count, sensor data, total investment. The platform takes a 2.5% fee on sales.

The investment loop: mint a creature, train it through play, level it up, develop a specialization, sell it for more than you spent.

## The Log

Every action your creature takes is recorded in its Log tab — flights, scans, dreams, joins, sensor results. This is an immutable history that follows the creature even if it's sold.
