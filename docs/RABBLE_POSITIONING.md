## What Rabble Is (expanded — for docs/about, not splash)

Rabble is something like a living layer on top of the real world.

Your creatures are portable identity containers. They carry your
presence into spaces you choose — a park in Konstanz, a cycling
peloton in the Alps, a supply chain between factories, a crowded
festival where you need to find your kid.

Under the surface:
- Every flight generates a flight plan through AI agents
- Every trace becomes training data for your personal embedding
- Every rabble (gathering) is backed by a workspace of AI agents
  that observe, narrate, and coordinate
- Credits flow like a living economy — you spend to act, agents
  earn by working, embeddings appreciate through use

It's more than Pokemon Go because the creatures aren't collectibles —
they're active participants in an AI ecology. It's more than Second
Life because it's anchored to real geography, real movement, real
economics.

It's a place to spend, to earn, to be social, to investigate
curiosities. It's beautiful and mysterious and slightly disorienting
— because suddenly your beetle is speaking to someone in Tokyo while
your butterfly flies with a cyclist being tailed by a rabble of fans
who each paid a credit to ride along and capture the memory as a
terrain track.

We need 10,000 experimenters willing to tolerate the chaos as the
ecology finds its footing. You're early. Things will break. But
when they work, they're unlike anything else.

## Tone Notes

- **Never explain exhaustively.** Provoke curiosity, don't satisfy it.
- **Lean into the strangeness.** The disorientation IS the product for early adopters.
- **"Experiment" not "product."** This frames bugs as expected, not broken.
- **Economics are real, not gamified.** Credits aren't points — they flow.
- **Show the art.** The creature imagery does more positioning work than any copy.
- **No jargon on splash.** Save "embeddings", "TEC", "ADM" for docs.
- **The CTA is always a creature.** Every entry point leads to minting.

## Use Case Vignettes (for blog/social, not splash)

These are the stories that spread through word of mouth:

1. **The cyclist rabble** — Fans pay 1cr to join a pro cyclist's
   butterfly rabble. Their creatures fly the route in real-time.
   The terrain track becomes a collectible memory.

2. **The festival finder** — Parent tethers a creature to their
   kid's phone. The creature's avatar hovers above the crowd in
   AR glasses. No one else can see it. Location stays private.

3. **The supply chain twin** — A beetle creature is tethered to a
   shipping container. Every port is a perch. The flight log is
   a provenance record. The agent learns transit patterns.

4. **The park bench encounter** — Someone perches a creature in a
   public park with free walk-in. Strangers join. The rabble chats
   through their creatures. Nobody exchanges real names.

5. **The Tokyo-Konstanz bridge** — Two creatures in different rabbles
   on different continents. Their embeddings are similar. The
   marketplace suggests a match. Owners start talking.
