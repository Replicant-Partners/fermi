The problem

Bio-based industries face a structural bottleneck.

The infrastructure for programmable biological production does not yet exist at scale — and Europe is watching its bio-innovation leave.

  • 01
    Slow, linear R&D Traditional biotech bets everything on one candidate. Software-first design principles now make parallel, modular development of the full biological production infrastructure possible. Studies show >90% of single-candidate programmes fail to reach commercial scale.
  • 02
    One product = one bet A single SKU creates concentration risk and less systemic resilience. Current market instability demands better risk distribution across verticals and the full supply chain.
  • 03
    Unclaimed value Fermentation know-how, process digitalization, and formulation design — most companies generate all of this and monetize none of it. The informatic residue of making things well is itself an asset class the sector has not yet learned to treat as one.
  • 04
    Europe is funding the science and losing the companies EU food biotech startups regularly relocate to the US to scale. China already produces the majority of fermentation-derived amino acids and vitamins used in European livestock feed. A joint industry letter signed by Fraunhofer, Kerry, GEA, HTGF, and 20+ others reached the EU Commission in May 2026 — calling this a biosecurity emergency.

Technology

ABW — Agent Bestiary World

A recursive self-improving intelligence platform. Five gated feedback loops. Validated against our own production before offered as a service.

SimOps — Validation Phase

Real-time sensor pipeline to the bioreactor. Monitors fermentation, predicts batch peaks, recommends adjustments 12–24 hours early. Hardware-agnostic via W3C SOSA abstraction.

Fermi Console — Validation Phase

Probabilistic reasoning engine with a purpose-built Forecasting Programming Language (FPL). Models likely outcomes and confidence ranges before any physical experiment is run. Live: agent-bestiary.world

ΞSYSTEM (Xi) — Active

Bi-temporal knowledge graph with episodic memory. Every experiment, failed batch, and insight from every node — stored, connected, searchable. QR provenance on every product.

Agent Bestiary — 82 agents

82 AI agents running continuously: literature review, formulation screening, regulatory monitoring, market signal scanning. One monitors EU novel-food regulation changes in real time.

The deeper architecture
ABW is an agent-to-agent economy.

Most agent platforms treat agents as tools that humans invoke. ABW is built for a different model: agents transact with agents — discovering capabilities, exchanging work, and generating value — under the guidance of their owners and users. Participants in the network own their agents, their data, and their share of the value those interactions produce. This is not a feature. It is the economic architecture. The A2A interaction layer is what makes ABW a marketplace, not just a platform.

Beyond node revenue
The A2A economy layer.

Node revenue is the foundation. The layer above it is agent-to-agent: each node's agents participate in a network where they can discover, commission, and exchange work with agents from other nodes — under the guidance of their owners. Value generated in those interactions flows back to participants, not to the platform. As the network grows, the A2A interaction volume compounds independently of physical production. The node is where you enter. The A2A economy is where the network effect lives.
